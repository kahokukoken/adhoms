## Evidence and finding

- Across trial years 2–5, the extractor found 1,152 displayed base seasonal FEED posts and only 288 distinct post texts: each shared seasonal text appeared four times. This count excludes conditional history/research rows produced by actual choice state. Those conditional rows do not remove the repeated base posts.
- Independent weekly AI first-reader reviewed all 24 months of years 2–3. Independent month-only AI first-reader reviewed all 24 months of years 4–5. Both understood the roles and some within-year consequences, but independently flagged the same recurrence as a high-priority continuity problem. This is simulated review, not human V1-14 acceptance.
- Examples: the exact April bus consultation and lunchbox episode return in following years; the November left-hand glove story repeats its discovery and resolution. March says “最初にバスの話を書いたの、もう去年の春” in multiple years, moving the first consultation forward. The following April repeats Matsumoto's first introduction despite the March handoff. Aoi remains 17 / 高校生 through multiple years. The meeting's “子どもの送迎バス” phrase can imply a bus carrying children, while Misaki needs the commuter bus after nursery drop-off.
- Positive continuity exists: spring's difference between a running bus and a usable trip is applied in winter; a limited neighbor ride leads to a support relationship without pretending the timetable is fixed; fuel-delivery constraints lead to a concrete shared receiving arrangement. These should be preserved during revision.

## Status and next production gate

Functional full-path testing is in place. Narrative continuity for years 2–5 is **not ready for a user full-play acceptance check**. The production side should revise the repeated seasonal baseline into year-specific states and ages, retain choice-conditioned reactions, then regenerate both reading routes, rerun independent readers and browser/standalone/save checks. Do not require the user to find the repetitive scenes by replaying four years. Human V1-14 judgment remains a final experiential gate after this internal pass; release and PR merge remain open.
