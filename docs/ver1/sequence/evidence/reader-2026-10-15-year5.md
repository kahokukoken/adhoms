The recorded March opening confirms that another hearing request was sent. This produces a small local reading stumble, not a false claim that a request was accepted or succeeded. Tanaka explicitly says that the *new March contact order* is the thing she has not personally tried, so her April experience does not erase earlier successful direct contact. - **No repeated first-time blocker:** April's one direct handover and May's first reply to an alternate contact can coexist. May identifies the missing absence/backup route and names the movement beyond forwarding to Minato. The later accounts explicitly refer back to earlier attempts, changed conditions, or renewed practice rather than pretending nobody has learned before.
- **Chronology:** April–July prepare August's two separate event sites and the shop/family location. August advances from morning to night and individual crisis, then explicitly opens September–March recovery. The eventual evaluation and private conversation are explicitly in March. The retrospective `下流ピークは未到達` line stays under an observation-history label; it does not require interpreting March as still on the disaster night. The annual count explicitly identifies eleven ordinary months plus the separately recorded August event.
- **Proposal versus confirmed result:** Ordinary-month headers and option descriptions keep request, acceptance, implementation, and improvement separate. August choices receive named counterpart replies scoped to the operational decision. Risk indicators are explicitly distinguished from confirmed survival, injury, and business loss. The narrative does not infer safety from a successful contact or deployment alone.
- **Consequences persist:** Both reviewed paths confirm Machi's survival without serious injury alongside major miso-business losses, Akio's survival with injury and delayed competitive return, and TOWA's survival without serious injury alongside delayed evacuation. September introduces these facts; October–March preserve them; the ending does not silently restore the shop or Akio's capabilities. The high weekly aggregate life-continuity score is explicitly prevented from erasing the individual loss.
- **Monthly comprehension:** The monthly sequence carries the main intermediate steps needed to understand handover delays, backup roles, receiving versus authorizing orders, current constraints on transport, rest and request boundaries, and the reduced record-writing trial. July's included voices retain the personal stakes for Machi and Akio before August.
- **Different visible paths remain internally legible:** The weekly packet establishes prepared portable shelters and a mobile command option; the monthly packet establishes different available resources and explicitly explains why portable deployment and mobile command deployment are unavailable. I did not assume the two packets represent identical prior choices, and their different aggregate outcomes are not in themselves contradictions.
- **Ending:** The individual and aggregate evaluations remain in tension rather than negating one another. The TOWA/T-0WA exchange leaves a mystery open; the epilogue labels that mystery as unexplained, so it is not presented as a missing fact the player should already have understood.
