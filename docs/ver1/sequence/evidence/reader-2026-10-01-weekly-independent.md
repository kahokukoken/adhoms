# Independent first-reader review: five-year weekly sequence

## Judgment

**FAIL on complete narrative continuity for this supplied playthrough.** The ordinary five-year town narrative is comprehensible and substantially continuous. The failure is concentrated in the final-day personal crisis and its connection to TOWA's eventual hospital conversation. Important outcome and decision-context information is missing from the actual sequence, so the reader has to invent part of the climax.

This is **first-reader AI review, not human acceptance**, a technical test result, or a judgment on unshown branches. It does not mean the entire story is incoherent. Most of the setup, relationships, annual learning, and post-disaster persistence of losses work.

## What I understood without outside explanation

The player is director Kiso Saku of 河北恒研, conducting a five-year ADHOMS field trial in 倶利伽羅町. The objective is to sustain the functions that make living possible through change, rather than maximize population or GDP. Residents report a partial view of the town, staff investigate and discuss what can be inferred, and the player makes policy decisions at major stages.

The core idea becomes concrete through recurring people and seasonal problems: a bus can run without being catchable after nursery drop-off; a shop can be open while its delivery entrance is flooded; an unlocked building can still depend on one unavailable keyholder; a delivery can arrive while its recipient cannot carry it indoors. Improvements can transfer work to another person. The records should help people continue a conversation without turning old help into perpetual consent.

The main relationships are legible:

- Kiso, Machi, and Shibagaki are childhood friends. Machi supplies miso and helps develop Murata's noodle dish; Shibagaki combines sumo operation with his own sporting life
- Toru is Kiso's university acquaintance and BRINE member. The amplifier repair leads naturally to the GENKAN song project
- Saya Miyashita is the analyst and Minato's sister. Minato is initially a resident/university student, not a research insider with automatic authority; that boundary is reinforced later
- Akari is Machi's younger sister, with her own activities and preferences rather than simply spare shop labor
- Residents such as Tanaka, Haruka, Kitamura, Sakamoto, Murata, Teranishi, Yamamoto, and Nishimura keep recognizable practical roles
- TOWA is a singer associated with biodiversity. Kiso first fails to recognize the name, then recognizes her voice, and the final conversation reveals a university connection and the name 永遠

The annual progression is also recognizable:

1. **Year 1:** Learn the town and distinguish an available service from an actually usable one
2. **Year 2:** Test what happens between receiving information and accomplishing an action
3. **Year 3:** Trace the work and lost time transferred to helpers by apparent improvements
4. **Year 4:** Make relationships and procedures usable without perpetual dependence on the original helper
5. **Year 5:** Test those accumulated arrangements in a compound disaster, then continue into recovery rather than treating survival as restoration

That is a real progression, not five identical years, even though the language and seasonal scaffolding repeat heavily.

## Blocking continuity defects

### B1. TOWA's emphasized danger has no stated outcome or bridge into the hospital scene

**Severity:** Blocking for the completed central-character sequence. **Confidence:** High.

Relevant sequence:

- **Year 3, December 2031:** Kiso says, 「この声は、知ってる。名前を見ただけじゃ、分からなかった。」 (`weekly-year3.md`, around lines 1182–1188)
- **Year 5, August 2033:** TOWA is repeatedly marked 「危険」, including the personal-crisis and convergence phases (`weekly-year5.md`, lines 573–686)
- **Year 5, August 2033, recovery transition:** 「確認された個別結果」 gives outcomes for Machi and Shibagaki only (lines 688–690)
- **Year 5, September 2033:** Fujii says, 「TOWAさんの状態は当日の結果に応じて確認を続けます。」 (line 703)
- **Year 5, March 2034, administrative evaluation:** 「個別残差」 again lists Machi and Shibagaki only (lines 1623–1625)
- **After that evaluation:** 「PRIVATE CONVERSATION / TOWA」「病院の面会スペース」, followed by TOWA speaking normally with Kiso (lines 1637 onward)

What is missing is not a full medical history. I cannot tell from the text whether TOWA was injured, hospitalized, recovered, or is visiting somebody else. Nor is there a stated point when her previously unresolved safety was confirmed. The hospital location strongly suggests a consequence, but never supplies it. The eventual dialogue confirms that she is alive at that point; it does not explain the intervening seven months or whose hospital visit this is.

The September wording is especially unhelpful as an in-world report: 「当日の結果に応じて」 refers to an outcome-dependent process instead of reporting what this run has established, or clearly stating what remains unknown and why. The other two named people receive explicit, route-specific outcomes and sustained follow-through; the third central crisis character does not.

This is **not a proven death/resurrection contradiction**. No supplied passage declares TOWA dead. It is a missing causal and status bridge.

Minimum repair: give this run's known TOWA outcome, or a specific and intentionally unresolved status, at recovery; update it before or at the hospital scene; identify why Kiso and TOWA are meeting there. Preserve uncertainty where appropriate, but do not require readers to infer injury or recovery merely from the building name. The revelation of 永遠 and the unresolved origin of T-0WA can remain separate mysteries.

### B2. The personal-priority decision announces a conflict without showing the actual competing situation

**Severity:** Blocking for understanding the climactic decision as a narrative choice. **Confidence:** High regarding the missing information; this is not a claim about hidden mechanics.

The July setup does useful work: it distinguishes the sumo venue, the forest-park event, and Machi's shop/family. Machi worries about leaving 「樽や帳簿」, and Shibagaki anticipates wanting to stay if someone remains. These are credible seeds.

But in **August 2033**, the decisive phase is only:

> 「高倉真知・柴垣晃生・TOWAの危機と町全体の優先順位が衝突。」
>
> 「個別優先判断：システム優先順位を維持」 / 「個別優先判断：手動で優先順位を変更」

(`weekly-year5.md`, lines 645–661)

The supporting text repeats the failed sumo-side route, the unreached downstream peak, numerical forecasts, available resources, and the three risk labels. It does not tell me the system's current priority, what help the three people now need, what a manual change would redirect, or which other people's urgent need would be delayed.

Consequently I understand the abstract theme—personal attachments versus town-wide priorities—but cannot describe the actual conflict the player is being asked to settle. “Maintain” has no narrated ordering to maintain. Afterward, Shibagaki's stranding/injury and Machi's business loss are plausible disaster outcomes, but most of the personal event arrives retrospectively as a result summary rather than as the crisis that motivated this decision.

Minimum repair: before the choice, provide a concise current report for each relevant location/person, the present resource assignment or priority, and the specific competing need. After the choice, state what actually happened. Numerical formulas or guaranteed outcomes are unnecessary; present-tense facts and uncertainties are enough.

## Other sequence problems worth correcting

### C1. The recovery transition still speaks as if the downstream peak is in the future

**Severity:** Moderate temporal clarity problem; not independently necessary for my FAIL judgment.

**August 2033** repeats 「上流域降水の流下を確認。下流ピークは未到達。」 in the night, personal-crisis, convergence, and recovery screens (`weekly-year5.md`, lines 629, 650, 668, 684). Immediately afterward the player proceeds to September.

The risk line is explicitly reframed as historical in recovery—「当日の避難上の危険度」—which is helpful. The downstream warning is not similarly dated. A first reader is left unsure whether the peak subsequently arrived, was managed, or is still an active unresolved threat when the game declares convergence.

Mark it as a historical nighttime observation and add one sentence closing the weather/event transition. There is no need to erase the original warning.

### C2. Tanaka's Year 5 opening can sound like a regression to a skill she already used

**Severity:** Moderate local ambiguity, not a definite contradiction.

- **Year 4, April 2032:** 「朝の連絡役、今年も私でいいかと聞かれました。」 She is an established intermediary, and Yamamoto's direct contact reduces her phone work
- **Year 4, March 2033:** 「私の名前を連絡役のまま残す所と、本人同士で話せる所が分かれました。」
- **Year 5, April 2033:** 「三月に引き継いだ朝の連絡、今週は私から送ってみます。…紙をもらって安心して、まだ自分では使っていなかった。」 (`weekly-year5.md`, line 17)

I can reconcile this as testing a newly revised process in the requester role after having coordinated the older one. The text does not actually name that distinction. Since the previous years repeatedly show her making calls and using written notes, “had not used it herself” momentarily resets the character.

Specify the new paper/process or the role change in a few words. Do not rewrite her as a novice to morning communication.

### C3. Some policy-to-resource transitions are asserted more than enacted

**Severity:** Moderate explanation opportunity, not a blocking contradiction.

Year 4 opens with four cooperation proposals and resistance from previous burdens. By **June 2032**, the available set is explicitly drone communications and school grounds. The sequence repeatedly distinguishes proposals from secured resources, and does not falsely claim that the warehouse and fuel agreements are fully available. That distinction works.

What remains thin is the concrete bridge explaining why these two proposals reached usable conditions and the other two did not under the selected relationship-repair policy. A short account of the actual reply or condition would make the change feel more earned than 「過去のRelationと4年目方針から、現在の手札が更新された」 alone. This matters because those exact resources return in the finale.

## Continuities that work and should be preserved

### The original bus problem is never silently solved

**April 2029** establishes an 8:00 nursery opening, a seven-minute walk, and an 8:05 bus. The Tuesday lift helps one day without changing the timetable. **March 2030** says 「本数はまだ同じ」. **April 2032** still distinguishes consultation with the operator from successful private lifts. **March 2034** keeps the consultation open while making its history usable.

This is deliberate unresolved infrastructure, not a dropped plot thread. A compulsory convenient timetable fix would undermine what the story has actually shown. The reader understands both the remaining problem and the narrower improvement.

### Seasonal returns generally add a new condition

Examples:

- Keys progress from one person being counted at two locations (**September 2029**), to two people waiting on opposite assumptions (**September 2030**), to opening versus closing responsibilities (**September 2031**), to a missing lighting instruction found by an independent user (**September 2032**)
- Winter access progresses from road versus doorstep (**January 2030**), to advance consultation without guaranteed accompaniment (**January 2031**), to burdens on accompanying relatives (**January 2032**), to another worker contacting the clinic directly (**January 2033**), to flood-altered entrances under snow (**January 2034**)
- Rice transport progresses from matching vehicle/driver/recipient time, to not loading before confirmation, to the driver's return home, to direct contact, to a post-flood shipment being stopped before an impossible handover

These are causally intelligible developments using earlier experience. They are not merely renamed copies of the original incidents.

### The creative side projects have visible provenance

Toru's **June 2029** amplifier visit sets up the **July** song discussion, live audience test, **August** alternate endings, and **September** report. Later references preserve those selected steps rather than claiming a different initial experiment. GENKAN stays a developing work, not an unexplained hit single.

Machi's **August 2029** invitation sets up **September** miso-noodle testing, the ten-serving workflow, **October** kitchen layout, and **November** shelf/serving improvement. Later years shift toward teaching the procedure. After the flood, the historical recipe is explicitly separated from current supply capacity. These are good small-scale continuity chains.

### Ages and the main youth timeline do not visibly contradict themselves

Minato goes from an eighteen-year-old university student through Years 2, 3, and 4 to post-university uncertainty. Aoi stops being labeled a high-school pupil after Year 1. Akari grows from nine-to-ten to twelve-to-thirteen over her appearances. The text does not suddenly give Minato administrative authority or Kaito a police job merely because they express interest.

Some adult life details are under-specified, especially Aoi's actual study/work situation and Ren's present educational or employment status. That limits attachment and the sense of five years passing, but the supplied text does not establish mutually incompatible facts. I would not “fix” this by inventing a graduation, job, or relationship that the sequence never confirms.

### Machi and Shibagaki's losses persist through recovery and the final evaluation

The recovery result and **September 2033** clearly state Machi survived without serious injury but lost equipment, the storehouse, and stock, and Shibagaki survived with an injury preventing an immediate sporting return. These are then carried through winter and **March 2034**.

Machi says, 「実証が終わっても、店の復旧は終わりじゃない。」 Shibagaki says, 「競技へ戻る時期は、まだ約束せん。戻らんと決めたわけでもない。」 Those lines preserve agency and incompleteness. The subsequent story does not simply put them back on the old workforce roster.

The distinction between a danger label and an eventual outcome is also explicit in the recovery screen. Shibagaki being labeled 注意 late in the event does not by itself contradict his later injury; risk is not a retrospective medical result.

### The TOWA recognition and AI-name mystery are distinguishable from the missing outcome

The sequence “unrecognized name → recognized voice → university recollection/name 永遠” is legible. The epilogue explicitly leaves T-0WA's name and voice origin unexplained. That is an intentionally open hook, not the same problem as omitting whether the singer survived the flood or why the final scene is at a hospital.

### The eleventh monthly record in Year 5 is not an omitted month

Year 5 explicitly contains August as the final-day sequence instead of a normal monthly observation meeting. The closing count of eleven monthly records is therefore understandable. All twelve calendar month headers are present in the file.

## Optional stylistic and emotional improvements

These are not prerequisites for the continuity judgment above:

1. **Reduce within-month re-explanation.** Many years contain an opening problem, dialogue discovering it, an end-of-month post restating it, and a meeting restating it again. This makes weekly reading feel longer than the actual amount of new information. Keep the changed fact and one good lived example rather than four formulations
2. **Let ordinary voices remain less programmatic.** Residents increasingly use the same careful distinctions about permission, uncertainty, and scope. The jokes about flowers, food, gloves, photographs, and siblings restore individuality and should stay. More differences in how people express the same insight would help
3. **Make the “came to understand” moments more concrete.** Repeated phrases such as 「相談を続けてきたので…」 correctly preserve history but can sound like explanatory inserts. A remembered shared action can do the same work more naturally
4. **Give Kiso one or two present-tense reactions to Machi and Shibagaki after the disaster.** Their childhood relationship is established, but much of the painful aftermath is handled by staff exposition. The silence at evaluation could carry more weight if it follows a small, specific encounter. This is emotional emphasis, not a demand for a romance or a tidy recovery
5. **Introduce Lena with a short situational line.** Her affiliation/role label makes her identifiable when she appears in May 2032, so I was not lost. A new-reader entry point explaining why she is involved now would avoid her feeling like a staff member the reader was supposed to remember
6. **Occasionally state concrete changes in the young adults' lives.** “My timetable is different” is consistent but thin when repeated over several years. Even modest, confirmed present-day details would make aging more tangible without forcing a definitive career ending

## What remains legitimately unresolved

These should not be treated as defects simply because the trial ends: the bus timing, unfilled help slots, people who have not responded, current resource agreements awaiting confirmation, Machi's business future, Shibagaki's sporting return, younger people's future plans, and T-0WA's explicitly withheld origin. The story repeatedly identifies these as open and provides a way to continue them.

An open issue becomes a continuity defect when an essential later scene depends on a changed state that has not been stated, or when a choice depends on a present situation that has not been shown. B1 and B2 cross those lines; the ordinary open consultations generally do not.

## Minimum evidence needed to change this judgment

Re-read the revised actual player-visible sequence, without relying on implementation or design intent, and establish that:

- Before the personal-priority choice, a reader can explain who needs what, what the existing priority is, and what other need competes with changing it
- All three emphasized personal-risk characters receive a known outcome or explicitly bounded unresolved status for this route
- The transition into the TOWA hospital conversation establishes the relevant status and reason for the setting, without requiring invented injury/recovery facts
- The disaster-to-recovery transition clearly distinguishes historical warnings from the current phase
- The existing month-to-month persistence of Machi's business loss and Shibagaki's injury is retained

Even after those repairs, an AI first-reader PASS would remain separate from a human play/read acceptance decision.
