## New or now-visible finding requiring correction

### N1 — Unavailable and undeployed portable shelter is called secured/deployed

**Concrete contradiction in this selected route; not an optional preference.**

The final-day morning screen (`year5:542`) says:

> 可搬避難所：機材提供の協定が未確認のため、展開は選べません。場所の提供とは分けて扱います。

The only portable-shelter choice is not to deploy. After that selection, `year5:567` replies:

> 「展開しない」を、確保済み可搬避難所の展開の範囲で引き受けます。

At night, equipment remains unavailable (`687`), and no deployment has occurred anywhere in the route. The neutral keep-as-is selection then produces `698`:

> 「現状維持」を、展開済み可搬避難所の再配置の範囲で引き受けます。

The first reply implies secured equipment despite explicit lack of agreement. The second implies actual deployment despite both unavailable equipment and the recorded no-deployment choice. A reader should not have to interpret “within the scope of already deployed shelters” as an empty set to reconcile ordinary narrative text.

Minimum correction: state that no portable-shelter deployment is being undertaken / no deployed portable shelter exists to move, and preserve the unconfirmed equipment state. Use a neutral decision acknowledgment rather than a generic acceptance formula that names nonexistent assets. This does not require granting resources or changing the selected result.
