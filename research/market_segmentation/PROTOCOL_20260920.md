# Domestic football market segmentation: descriptive discovery audit

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: data collection, not a betting signal.

Question approved in conversation: do participation restrictions, supporter motivations,
and differing information access mean that domestic Japanese football pools behave
unlike overseas prices, even for the same match?

Scope fixed before aggregate calculation: all official toto draw IDs in the 2025
result index with results dated 2025-09-01 through 2025-12-31. Products 01 (toto),
06 (mini A), and 07 (mini B). The official index lists these IDs:
1565,1566,1567,1568,1570,1571,1572,1574,1575,1576,1577,1578,1579,1580,1582,
1583,1585,1586,1587,1588,1589,1591,1592,1593,1595,1596,1597,1598.
Unavailable product-draw pages remain coverage gaps, not losing observations to delete.

Primary descriptive contrasts:
1. Within the SAME match, compare toto versus mini shares. Use vote counts rather
   than rounded displayed percentages. Check all counts sum to the product total.
2. Compare domestic shares with de-vigged overseas 1X2 odds only for exact
   date/home/away identity matches. Football-Data.co.uk is a provider benchmark,
   not ground truth. Check goals/results agree and report unmatched rows.
3. Report full-vector absolute distance, favorite concentration, draw allocation,
   match-level Brier score and log loss, and date-block uncertainty where meaningful.
   Different products and repeated listings of the same match are NOT independent.
4. Stratify domestic J1 versus non-Japanese competitions if sufficient matched
   data are available; keep J2/J3 unlabelled until identity/competition is verified.
5. Supporter identity/intent, nationality, money by bettor type, true information
   availability and identical decision timestamps are NOT observed in public totals.
   Do not infer a causal fan effect or a nationality effect from share differences.

Outcome results, final votes and overseas closing prices are available retrospectively.
This audit is discovery/descriptive, NOT an untouched out-of-sample experiment.
A match's overseas closing price may post-date the entire toto draw's sales cutoff,
especially for games held the following day. It MUST NOT be presented as an
executable purchase-time signal. No ROI/CLV/ruin estimate without a separate
notification-and-settlement replay and timestamped source audit.

For later causal tests, pre-register lagged fan proxies (not end-of-season future
attendance), promotions published before cutoff, news timing, team strength,
home advantage, budget/turnover, product mechanics and multi-ticket expansion.
No hard-coded Japanese/foreign rationality parameter.

Payout mechanics: WINNER is a score-bin pool, not directly a 1X2 wager. A sum of
score-bin inverse prices is not an executable home-win price. Toto/mini are
multi-match pools; marginal vote products need not equal combination demand.
Account for single/multiple tickets, correlations, carryover, prize tiers, rounding,
refunds and the entrant's own effect on payout. Do not subtract takeout twice.

Existing research/football model locks and boat workflows remain untouched.
No real-money purchase, account signup, deposit, paid dataset contract or recurring
workflow is authorized by this audit. The acquisition workflow is a one-shot probe.
