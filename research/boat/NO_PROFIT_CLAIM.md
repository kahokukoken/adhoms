# No Profit Claim Lock

As of 2026-09-19, ADHOMS BOATRACE research has not established profitability.

## Current permitted statement

BOATRACE is a promising public-data research target because official pages expose entrant, before-info, odds, result, and payout data under stable race identifiers.

## Current prohibited statements

Do not say:

- BOATRACE is profitable.
- ADHOMS can currently make money from BOATRACE.
- 50,000 JPY can currently be grown reliably via BOATRACE.
- Closing-odds backtest profit proves executable profit.

## Required evidence before changing this lock

All of the following must pass:

1. Parser Gate 0.
2. Historical closing-odds upper-bound test.
3. Fixed out-of-sample validation.
4. Forward paper-trading with pre-close snapshots.
5. Bankroll simulation for 50,000 JPY.
6. Drawdown and ruin-probability checks.
7. Venue/month/bet-type concentration checks.

Until then, all work is research and validation only.
